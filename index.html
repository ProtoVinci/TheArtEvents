The studio

We compose celebrations the way an artist composes a canvas.

Our approach

Express. Inspire. Create.

The backdrop is the photograph everyone keeps. So we design it like one — a single palette, a single story, and every detail considered.

  1. 01

    Express

    We begin with what makes the celebration yours — the name, the story, the colours you love.

  2. 02

    Inspire

    A palette, a backdrop and a lighting plan that turn an ordinary room into a place worth photographing.

  3. 03

    Create

    Balloon sculpture, printed panels, cut-outs and neon — built and installed by one team.
